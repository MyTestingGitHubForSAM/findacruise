US4 · Select a month range

Find sailings within a suitable travel period.

Story 4 — Select a departure month range

As a consumer, I want to select a departure month range, so that I can search for cruises sailing during a period that works for me.

Dependencies: US1 · calendar availability and date decisions

Scope: Home Page component; illustrative data and existing design-system assumptions.

Why it matters

Find sailings within a suitable travel period. Unavailable months, provisional starts, completed ranges, same-month, earlier-month and cross-year behavior.

Acceptance criteria

US4 · AC1#

AC1 — Display the four-year period

Scenario: Display the departure month selector

Given the current calendar year is 2026

When the consumer opens the Departure Date filter

Then the selector displays 2026, 2027, 2028 and 2029

And each year displays its twelve calendar months

US4 · AC2#

AC2 — Differentiate available and unavailable months

Scenario: Prevent selection of a month without available sailings

Given the search capability indicates that June 2028 has no available sailings

When the Departure Date filter is displayed

Then June 2028 is presented as unavailable

And the consumer cannot select June 2028

Temporary assumption: month availability is global and does not change according to the destination or departure port selections.

US4 · AC3#

AC3 — Select the start month

Scenario: Select the first month of the range

Given May 2027 has available sailings

And no departure month has been selected

When the consumer selects May 2027

Then May 2027 becomes the start month

And the selector remains open for the end-month selection

US4 · AC4#

AC4 — Complete the range

Scenario: Select a valid departure month range

Given May 2027 is the selected start month

And September 2027 has available sailings

When the consumer selects September 2027

Then September 2027 becomes the end month

And the inclusive range is May 2027 through September 2027

And the filter displays "May '27 - Sep '27"

US4 · AC5#

AC5 — Select a range across calendar years

Scenario: Select a range spanning two calendar years

Given November 2027 is the selected start month

When the consumer selects February 2028

Then the range is November 2027 through February 2028

And the filter displays both boundary months

US4 · AC6#

AC6 — Select an earlier second month

This behavior requires Product Owner confirmation.

Scenario: Select a month earlier than the current start month

Given September 2027 is the selected start month

When the consumer selects May 2027

Then May 2027 becomes the new start month

And no end month is selected

US4 · AC7#

AC7 — Open only one filter panel at a time

Scenario: Open this filter while another filter is open

Given another filter panel is open

When the consumer opens this filter

Then the previously open panel closes

And any existing selection in that filter remains unchanged

And only this filter panel remains open

AC8 — Past months remain visible but disabled

Given the calendar is open and the current demonstration month is September 2026

When the consumer views months before September 2026

Then January through August 2026 remain visible and disabled

And they cannot become a range start or end or alter an existing selection

And September 2026 and future months are selectable only when sailings are available

Questions and decisions

Temporary assumptions are not confirmed answers. Blocking decisions must be resolved before production Engineering.

Blocking questions and notes

Blocking Open

Does “four-year calendar” mean four calendar years or a rolling 48-month period?

Decision owner / domain
Product Owner
Temporary assumption
It means the current calendar year plus the following three calendar years.
Source reasoning
This determines which months are displayed and requested.
Effect of change · delivery assessment
Revisit the affected US4 behavior and acceptance criteria; review scope, dependencies, prototype behavior, test coverage and UAT before adopting a changed answer.
Blocking Open

Can the same month be selected as both start and end?

Decision owner / domain
Product Owner
Temporary assumption
Yes. Selecting the same month twice creates a one-month range.
Source reasoning
Consumers may want to search within one month.
Effect of change · delivery assessment
Revisit the affected US4 behavior and acceptance criteria; review scope, dependencies, prototype behavior, test coverage and UAT before adopting a changed answer.
Blocking Open

What happens when the second selected month is earlier than the first?

Decision owner / domain
Product Owner / UX Designer
Temporary assumption
The second selection becomes the new start and the previous partial selection is cleared.
Source reasoning
This defines a central transition in the selector state model.
Effect of change · delivery assessment
Revisit the affected US4 behavior and acceptance criteria; review scope, dependencies, prototype behavior, test coverage and UAT before adopting a changed answer.
Blocking Open

What happens if the consumer exits after selecting only a start month?

Decision owner / domain
Product Owner / UX Designer
Temporary assumption
An incomplete range is not applied and the filter continues to represent Any Date.
Source reasoning
Development must know whether a single selection is a valid criterion.
Effect of change · delivery assessment
Revisit the affected US4 behavior and acceptance criteria; review scope, dependencies, prototype behavior, test coverage and UAT before adopting a changed answer.
Blocking Open

Does month availability depend on the selected destination and departure port?

Decision owner / domain
Product Owner / Technical Lead
Temporary assumption
Month availability is global. A month is selectable when at least one sailing is available during that month, independently of the other filter selections.
Source reasoning
Contextual availability would introduce dependencies between filters, recalculation rules and handling for selections that become invalid. None of this behavior is defined in the requirement.
Effect of change · delivery assessment
Revisit the affected US4 behavior and acceptance criteria; review scope, dependencies, prototype behavior, test coverage and UAT before adopting a changed answer.
