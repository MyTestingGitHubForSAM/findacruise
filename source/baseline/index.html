A five-story proposal that brings cruise discovery to the Home Page — with clear boundaries, explicit decisions and executable evidence.

THE PRODUCT PROBLEM

Make the first step easier.

Consumers need a direct way to reach relevant cruise options from the Royal Caribbean Home Page. The proposed outcome is less friction, using destination, departure port and travel months.

Proposed MVP

A search component on the Home Page that lets consumers choose a destination, departure port or travel period, then continue to cruise results with their selected preferences applied.
