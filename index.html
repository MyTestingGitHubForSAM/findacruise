Find a Cruise

A Home Page search experience for Royal Caribbean consumers.

The problem

Consumers need a direct way to find cruises that match where they want to go, where they can depart from and when they want to travel.

The proposed solution

Bring destination, departure port and travel-month selection together on the Home Page, then carry those preferences into cruise results.
